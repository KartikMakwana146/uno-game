About

uno.io is a free online multiplayer card game inspired by the legendary skribbl.io aesthetic.


Match cards by color or number, play tricky action cards like Skip, Reverse, and Draw Two, and shout UNO before your rivals catch you!


First player to empty their hand wins the round. Have fun!

How to play

1. MATCH CARDS!

When it's your turn, play a card matching either the active color or number of the top discard card.

2. ACTION CARDS!

Use Skip to block your opponent, Reverse to switch turns, and Draw Two (+2) to make the next player draw cards!

3. WILD & +4!

Wild cards can be played on any turn to change the color. Wild Draw 4 forces the next player to draw 4 cards!

4. SAY UNO!

When you have only 1 card left in your hand, press the big red UNO! button immediately!

5. WIN THE ROUND!

The first player to discard all their cards wins the round and scores points from remaining opponents' cards!
